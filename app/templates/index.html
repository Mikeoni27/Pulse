How revenue attribution works

When someone clicks your Pulse link, we append a pulse_click_id to the destination URL. When they complete a Stripe checkout, if your app passes that ID back as client_reference_id, we match the payment to the original click.

This means:

  • ✅ Works for: your own signup/checkout flow (you control it)
  • ✅ Works for: any site where you can add a Stripe client_reference_id
  • ❌ Doesn't work for: third-party sites you don't control
